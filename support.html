Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Where is my data stored?

Shift records and settings are stored locally on your device. ShiftLog does not require an account and does not send this data to a ShiftLog server.

How do I add or edit a shift?

Open the Shifts tab and tap the plus button to add a shift. Tap an existing shift to edit its date, times, break, workplace, or notes.

How do I delete my data?

In the Shifts list, swipe a shift to the left to delete it. To remove all ShiftLog data stored by the app, uninstall the app. Device or cloud backups are controlled separately by your operating system provider.

How do I export a PDF report?

Open Reports, choose the month, and tap Export monthly PDF. The report is created on your device, then the system share sheet lets you choose where to save or send it.

Why does my estimated pay differ from payroll?

ShiftLog calculates an estimate using your hourly rate, standard daily hours, and overtime multiplier. Taxes, allowances, deductions, special rates, and employer rules may not be included. Your employer's payroll statement is the official source.

Can I restore deleted shifts?

ShiftLog does not currently include an undo or cloud restore feature. Export reports you need to keep before deleting records or uninstalling the app.
