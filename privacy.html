Privacy Policy

Effective: June 9, 2026

ShiftLog is designed to work without an account or backend server. This policy explains what information the app stores and the choices you control.

✓

Privacy at a glance

  • No account
  • No data collection
  • No ads or tracking
  • Local device storage

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the ShiftLog mobile application for iOS and Android ("ShiftLog", "we", "our", or "the app"). For privacy questions, contact caovanduong20@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter

ShiftLog lets you enter information such as employee name, workplace, hourly rate, overtime settings, shift dates and times, break duration, and notes. This information is stored locally on your device for the app features you request.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect or receive your personal data or app content. ShiftLog does not require sign-in and does not operate a backend server for user data.

  • No contact, location, health, or financial account data is collected
  • No analytics or advertising SDK is included
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking is performed
  • No sensitive device permissions are requested

4. How local information is used

The app processes information on your device to save and display shifts, calculate work hours and overtime, estimate pay based on your settings, and generate monthly PDF reports.

Pay figures are estimates for reference only and do not replace official payroll records.

5. PDF export and third-party services

PDF reports are generated on your device. When you choose to save or share a report, your operating system displays available apps and services. Any destination you select, such as email, messaging, file storage, or a cloud provider, handles the report under its own terms and privacy policy. ShiftLog does not control those services.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Shift records and settings remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear the app's storage where supported, or uninstall the app. To delete one shift, swipe it to the left in the Shifts list.

Your operating system or device provider may include app data in a device or cloud backup if you enable backups. Backup retention and deletion are controlled by that provider through your device or account settings.

7. Data security

Because your information is stored locally, protecting it depends in part on your device security. Use a device passcode, keep your operating system updated, and take care when exporting or sharing reports. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

ShiftLog is a work-record utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

ShiftLog does not transfer app data to our servers in any country because we do not collect or receive that data. Information you choose to share with another service is handled according to that service's practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ShiftLog features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
